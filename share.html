Share Your DeltaChat ID

Create a personal link to share with others so they can contact you easily.

Use letters, numbers, and hyphens. Min 3 characters.

How to get your DeltaChat invite link:
  1. Go to DeltaChat Settings.
  2. Tap the QR code icon at the top.
  3. Select Share QR Code.
  4. Then choose Copy link.

Only web links starting with https://i.delta.chat/# will work.

Privacy Notice

Your invite link is stored in our database so others can contact you. Anyone with your link can see this information.
