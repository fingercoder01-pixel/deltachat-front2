Privacy Policy

🔒 We Don't Know Anything About You

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your IP address
  • Your phone number
  • Your real name
  • Your location
  • Your contact list

🔐 End-to-End Encryption

All messages are encrypted with PGP encryption before leaving your device. We cannot read your messages even if we wanted to, because we don't have the decryption keys.

🗑️ No Storage

Messages are automatically deleted from our servers after the retention period expires. We don't keep logs or maintain any permanent records of your communication.

📋 Key Principles

  • Messages expire automatically after retention period
  • Unencrypted messages are rejected
  • IP addresses are not stored or linked to accounts
  • No data sharing with third parties
  • Full compliance with privacy regulations

Summary: We act like a postal service for encrypted packages. We don't know what's inside, and we're not interested in keeping copies. Your privacy is our priority.
